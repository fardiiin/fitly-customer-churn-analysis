# Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Strategy — Fit.ly Tech

## Project Overview

An end-to-end customer analytics case study investigating subscription cancellation patterns for a fictional fitness technology company.

The objective was to examine customer account information, support interactions, and product activity to identify patterns associated with cancellation and translate the findings into actionable retention recommendations.

## My Approach

- Integrated three datasets covering customer accounts, support interactions, and user activity.
- Conducted data profiling, validation, and cleaning.
- Performed exploratory data analysis using Python and Pandas.
- Examined cancellation-flag patterns across subscription plans, engagement levels, and customer-support resolution times.
- Developed business recommendations and presented the findings in an executive-style report and presentation.

## Key Areas of Investigation

- Customer engagement and cancellation patterns.
- Customer-support resolution performance.
- Subscription plan comparisons.
- Customer segmentation and retention opportunities.
- Data quality and measurement limitations.

## Tools & Technologies

Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Jupyter Notebook

## Business Recommendations

Prioritize investigation of prolonged support-resolution times, introduce proactive engagement initiatives for inactive users, and establish more reliable cancellation and retention KPIs.

*Note: This is an educational case study using supplied assessment data. The findings represent associations in the available dataset, not established causal relationships.*
